Pipeline Trust Decay Model™
DEFINITION

The Pipeline Trust Decay Model™ measures the rate at which candidate and talent market trust erodes as a direct consequence of information asymmetry in hiring pipelines.

It quantifies the compounding organizational cost of one-directional information flow — and provides a structured protocol for reversing it before the damage becomes permanent.

THE TRUST ASYMMETRY LAW

“In any hiring pipeline where information flows primarily inward without corresponding outward flow, trust decays at an accelerating rate. This decay is not linear — it follows a compounding curve that becomes increasingly costly to reverse.”

Corollary: The cost of reducing candidate uncertainty today is always lower than the cost of recovering trust tomorrow.

FOUR DECAY MECHANISMS
📡
Signal Inflation
SII
Systematic dilution of candidate signal quality caused by uncertainty-driven application behavior. When candidates cannot predict outcomes, they apply indiscriminately — making qualified applicants statistically indistinguishable from unqualified ones.
Qualified Applications ÷ Total Applications
Cause: Absent outbound communication
Effect: Recruiter screening efficiency collapses
🔄
Decision Debt
DDR
Accumulated redundant evaluative work created when prior hiring decisions are not converted into reusable organizational knowledge. Each undocumented decision becomes future duplicate labor.
Returning Pipeline Entries ÷ Total Pipeline Entries
Cause: ATS used as storage, not knowledge infrastructure
Effect: Recruiters solve the same problems every cycle
📉
Trust Depreciation
TDR
Progressive erosion of employer brand equity when candidate experiences are negative, undocumented, or unaddressed. Trust depreciation is cumulative and does not reverse without deliberate intervention.
Negative Interactions ÷ Total Candidate Interactions
Cause: No candidate experience measurement
Effect: Future pipeline quality and response rates decline
🔗
Referral Leakage
RLR
Invisible reduction in future talent access caused by ignored candidates removing themselves and their networks from an organization’s referral ecosystem. The highest-value cost — never measured, compounds indefinitely.
Proxy: Referral rate trend over 4+ quarters
Cause: Zero feedback to non-hired candidates
Effect: Top-of-funnel quality deteriorates invisibly quarter over quarter
