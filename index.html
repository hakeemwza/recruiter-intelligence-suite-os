Overview

What Is RIS?

The Recruiter Intelligence Suite is a multi-engine AI framework that encodes veteran recruiter judgment into structured prompts and scoring systems. It works on top of the tools you already use — ATS, LinkedIn, email — and produces scored, actionable output at every stage of the hiring process.

RIS is not software to install. There is no integration, no onboarding ticket, no IT approval. It is an intelligence layer applied to real recruiting workflows. Paste the prompt. Feed it the job brief and the candidate. Get a scored output in under three minutes.

Eight engines. One framework. Every placement decision backed by structure, not gut feeling.

Frequently Asked Questions

What AI tools does RIS work with? +

RIS prompts are compatible with any large language model including Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot. No specific tool is required — if you can paste text and read an output, you can use RIS.

Do I need to be a technical recruiter to use this? +

No. RIS is written for working recruiters. The prompts are plain language. The outputs are plain language. If you can paste text and read a scored output, you can use RIS from day one.

How long does it take to get a result? +

Most RIS engines return a full scored output in under 3 minutes when given a complete job description and resume. The Quick Start Guide gets you a live diagnostic in under 15 minutes on your first use.

Can I use RIS for retained and contingency search? +

Yes. The scoring and risk frameworks apply to both models. The Deal Health Engine is particularly useful for retained mandates where placement failure carries higher financial cost and the intervention window is narrower.
